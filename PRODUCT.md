# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router) + Tailwind CSS v4. Chosen by the owner.

## Users

- People in Bogotá looking for a café, bar and cultural space that feels different: students, artists, couples, groups of friends, curious visitors. All ages and publics are welcome; the space is meant to be unsettling in mood, never exclusionary.
- Visitors arriving from Instagram who want to know what the place is, see the menu, and find out how to get there and what is happening this week.

## Product Purpose

El Purgatorio is a café, cocktail bar and "casa de cultura" inside a section of a castle in Bogotá (Castillo del Mono Osorio, Cl. 74 #02-86, as printed on the venue's own event poster). Tagline: "Arte, café y algo más". The website introduces the space, shows the menu, promotes events and workshops, and drives visits.

## Positioning

A café inside a real castle, intervened as an art space. Its imagery (the veiled red figure in the logo, scarred and dismembered dolls, the UV-lit gallery) points at the suffering women carry silently, not at women as objects of sensuality. Bizarre, slightly terrifying, but welcoming to everyone.

## Operating Context

- Interior: raw stone castle arches, walls painted blood red and white, a UV/blacklight gallery room where paintings and dolls glow neon, a bar with a handwritten neon-style "memento mori" wall, courtyard terrace with string lights and plants.
- Programme seen on the venue's posts: tarot nights ("Oráculo de cristal"), blind-drawing workshop ("Incomódate"), skull-painting kits ("Ecos de un amor después de la muerte"), fire performances, couples' workshops.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Menu (from `menu_purgatorio_vino_qr.pdf`): signature cocktails named after the seven deadly sins (Lujuria, Pereza, Gula, Envidia, Soberbia, Avaricia, Ira) plus classics, wine, beer, coffee, cold drinks, pastry, snacks, sandwiches, soups, mains. Prices in COP.
- UNDECIDED (do not invent): opening hours, Instagram handle, WhatsApp/phone number, reservation method, event calendar dates. Render as clearly marked fields for the owner to fill.

## Brand Commitments

- Logo: veiled figure holding a small figure, blood red on black, with the "EL PURGATORIO" wordmark in a white condensed face on a black (or red) bar.
- Colours set by the owner: blood red, white, neon details. Gothic but modern and clean.
- Script tagline "Arte, café y algo más".
- Spanish (Colombia) copy.

## Evidence on Hand

- Owner photos in repo root (`2.jpeg`–`7.jpeg`, `WhatsApp Image …jpeg`) and Instagram exports (`Instagram.zip`, `Instagram (1).zip`).
- Menu PDF with real items and prices.
- No testimonials, press, or ratings supplied: none may be fabricated.

## Product Principles

1. Unsettle, then welcome: the mood is dark, the door is open to everyone.
2. The woman is witness, not ornament: imagery speaks of hidden pain, never of sensuality.
3. The castle is real: show the stone, the arches and the rooms, not a costume of them.
4. Every price and fact on the site comes from the owner's material.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

All-ages public: avoid graphic gore and explicit imagery; horror is atmospheric. Honour reduced motion; keep text contrast at WCAG AA on dark grounds.
